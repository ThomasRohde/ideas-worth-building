# Clear human-readable title

> One sentence that preserves the core proposition without turning it into a slogan.

## The spark

What genuinely prompted the proposal? Preserve the origin rather than rewriting history into a cleaner narrative.

## The proposition

What may be worth creating or exploring? State the response and its boundaries.

## Who or what it serves

Identify the people, communities, institutions, practices, ecosystems, bodies of knowledge, technical systems, or desired experiences that may benefit.

## Why it matters

Explain what could change and why that change is meaningful. Do not manufacture urgency, scale, demand, or pain.

## Current behaviours and alternatives

Describe what happens now, including workarounds, existing products, adjacent practices, deliberate non-use, and reasons the current state persists.

## The non-obvious insight

State the mechanism, synthesis, tension, constraint, or point of view that makes this proposal distinctive.

## What would make an implementation excellent

Describe the qualities, interactions, boundaries, craft, safeguards, and exclusions that would separate an excellent implementation from a merely functional one.

## Evidence, assumptions, and speculation

Separate externally supported claims, first-hand observations, assumptions, and speculation. Cite sources close to the claims they support.

## Known uncertainties and strongest counterargument

Keep the important unknowns visible. State the strongest good-faith case against the proposal and what evidence could change the judgment.

## Risk of solutionism

Explain whether a technology, capability, or preferred solution is leading the reasoning and how that risk is being controlled.

## Smallest meaningful exploration

Describe the least effort that can produce decision-relevant learning or reveal the intended experience.

## Promotion case

Explain why human curators judged this proposal worth preserving now. Address meaning, grounding, distinctiveness, excellence, and learning without reducing the decision to a score.
