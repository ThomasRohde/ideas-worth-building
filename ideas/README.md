# Curated ideas

This directory is the repository's curated memory.

Each document began as a `type:proposal` Issue, developed through research, critique, synthesis, or experiment, and crossed a human-controlled promotion boundary through a pull request. Promotion means the proposal is worth preserving as a serious and distinctive candidate for creation or further exploration. It is not a guarantee of novelty, feasibility, safety, demand, funding, or success.

## What belongs here

A promoted document must:

- link to its source Issue;
- identify who or what it serves and why that matters;
- represent alternatives and evidence honestly;
- make its non-obvious insight legible;
- describe the qualities required for excellent execution;
- retain uncertainties and the strongest counterargument;
- propose a smallest meaningful exploration;
- explain why human curators chose to preserve it;
- credit material contributors and disclose material agent assistance.

The exact schema is defined in [`SCHEMA.md`](../SCHEMA.md). Start from [`_template.md`](_template.md).

## What does not belong here

Do not add:

- raw Issue exports;
- generic generated idea lists;
- proposals that have not completed human promotion review;
- implementation plans detached from an idea's meaning and quality bar;
- rankings or scores presented as curation decisions.

## Naming

Use a short, durable, lowercase kebab-case filename:

```text
ideas/context-preserving-work-handoff.md
```

Avoid product names that may change. The title in front matter may remain expressive.
