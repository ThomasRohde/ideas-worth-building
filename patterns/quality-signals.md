# Quality signals near the promotion boundary

These signals help reviewers reason. They are not a checklist whose completion guarantees promotion, and they must not be converted into an autonomous score.

## Meaning

- The proposal identifies who or what it serves without inventing a persona.
- The claimed benefit is concrete enough to contest.
- The proposal explains why the change matters beyond convenience or novelty.
- The ambition is proportionate: it neither hides behind vagueness nor inflates itself into a universal solution.

## Grounding

- Current behaviour and credible alternatives are represented fairly.
- “Do nothing” or continue the workaround is considered when relevant.
- Verifiable claims are sourced; sources support the actual claims made.
- Facts, observations, assumptions, preferences, and speculation are distinguishable.
- Material counterevidence is not omitted because it weakens the pitch.

## Distinctiveness

- The proposal contains a non-obvious mechanism, synthesis, constraint, beneficiary, or experiential point of view.
- Removing the fashionable technology does not erase the entire idea.
- The proposal can explain why superficially similar alternatives miss something important.
- Its boundaries are chosen, not accidental.

## Excellence

- The quality bar describes what excellent execution would feel or behave like, not only what features it contains.
- Important exclusions and trade-offs are visible.
- Safety, privacy, accessibility, maintenance, incentives, and failure behaviour are considered where material.
- The proposal demonstrates judgment about where complexity belongs and where it must not leak.

## Learning

- The largest uncertainties are named.
- The strongest counterargument is treated seriously.
- A small exploration could materially change the decision.
- Experiment success criteria include learning and disconfirmation, not only engagement or technical completion.

## Stewardship

- The Issue body reflects the current synthesis rather than forcing newcomers to reconstruct it from comments.
- Related and possibly duplicate proposals are linked.
- Material contributors are credited.
- Agent involvement and remaining uncertainty are disclosed.

A proposal may show all these signals and still not be promoted. Human curators may judge that it is insufficiently meaningful, distinctive, timely, or aligned with the repository's quality bar. That judgment should be explained rather than hidden behind a number.
