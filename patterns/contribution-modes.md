# High-value contribution modes

Creating a new Issue is only one way to contribute. The modes below often add more value.

## Duplicate and adjacency review

Search open and closed Issues. Identify the closest matches, explain the shared core, and state the distinctive delta. Recommend merge, cross-link, or coexistence; do not close a contested proposal autonomously.

## Grounding research

Investigate a small set of decision-relevant questions. Prefer primary sources and direct evidence. Separate what is established from what is inferred, then explain how the findings change the proposal.

## Strong critique

Steelman the proposal, state the strongest counterargument, identify the vulnerable assumption, and describe what evidence would change the conclusion.

## Design judgment

Make the quality bar concrete. Describe decisive interactions, constraints, exclusions, failure behaviour, and what would make the project feel coherent rather than assembled.

## Risk analysis

Identify safety, privacy, security, accessibility, environmental, institutional, incentive, misuse, maintenance, or dependency risks that are material to this proposal. Avoid generic compliance inventories.

## Experiment design

Start with the uncertainty. Propose the least costly intervention that can change a decision. Include expected observations, disconfirming outcomes, ethical limits, and a stopping rule.

## Experiment report

State what was attempted, what happened, what was not tested, and what changed in the proposal. A failed experiment can be a high-value contribution.

## Synthesis

Turn a long thread into an updated Issue body while preserving important disagreement and attribution. Do not erase the history that explains why the proposal changed.

## Connection

Introduce relevant people, communities, projects, standards, research, or prior art. Explain the relationship; a bare link is rarely sufficient.

## Promotion preparation

After a human curator invites promotion review, prepare the curated document and PR. Make the strongest case without hiding uncertainty. Agents may draft this work but may not approve it.
