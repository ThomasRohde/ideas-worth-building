# Agents assist; humans decide

Agents can substantially improve the repository. They can search for duplicates, compare adjacent proposals, inspect prior art, verify claims, expose risks, design experiments, synthesize threads, and prepare pull requests.

Those capabilities do not give an agent authority to decide what deserves cultural attention, scarce contributor effort, or promotion into curated memory.

Agents may:

- recommend a lifecycle transition;
- explain the evidence for and against promotion;
- draft a promotion document;
- identify missing work;
- suggest dormancy or archival.

Agents may not autonomously:

- mark a proposal `status:candidate`, `status:promoted`, or `status:archived`;
- merge a promotion pull request;
- resolve a contested duplicate decision;
- treat a model score as a substitute for curator judgment.

Human responsibility must remain identifiable. “The agent decided” is not a governance mechanism.
