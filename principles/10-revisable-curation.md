# Curated memory is revisable

Promotion means “worth preserving now,” not “eternally correct,” “guaranteed to succeed,” or “owned by this repository.”

A promoted idea may later be:

- refined through follow-up pull requests;
- connected to new evidence or experiments;
- superseded by a better formulation;
- retired because its assumptions failed;
- archived because the environment changed;
- revived when new capabilities or needs appear.

Revision should preserve history rather than silently rewriting it. Material changes should link the relevant Issue or decision record. Retired curated documents move to `archive/` with a reason and, when applicable, a successor link.

Curation is a maintained judgment, not a frozen canon.
