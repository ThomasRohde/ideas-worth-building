# Preserve lineage and share credit

Ideas rarely have a single clean origin. They evolve through observations, prior work, conversation, critique, recombination, and experiments.

Contributors should:

- link related open and closed Issues;
- identify relevant prior art without using it to dismiss all further exploration;
- credit material human contributions in promoted documents;
- disclose material agent assistance;
- preserve dissent that changed the proposal, even when the final synthesis takes another direction;
- distinguish “independently reached” from “novel.”

Attribution is not only courtesy. It helps readers understand how the idea changed and where its strongest reasoning came from.

Do not claim ownership of a broad problem space or familiar concept. Do not use attribution demands to obstruct legitimate recombination and critique.
