# Depth over volume

The repository does not optimize for the number of Issues, comments, reactions, contributors, or generated concepts.

Idea generation is already abundant. Unfiltered abundance raises the cost of attention and makes genuinely unusual proposals harder to notice. A contribution should leave the repository with more signal than it had before.

Prefer:

- improving one existing proposal over filing a nearby duplicate;
- one decision-relevant research note over a broad literature dump;
- one precise counterargument over generic skepticism;
- one revealing experiment over a large prototype;
- one careful synthesis over many repetitive comments.

Agents should not produce “50 ideas” unless a human has explicitly requested a bounded divergence exercise and the output will be curated before entering the repository. Volume is an intermediate technique, never the repository's product.
